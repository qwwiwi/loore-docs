| `yt_video` | `POST /v1/yt-video` | Read one YouTube video or Short: title, full description, channel, views, likes, comments, duration, publish date, genre, keywords, chapters, the paid-promotion and made-with-AI flags, and captionLanguages (the subtitle languages yt_transcript can return). |
